How we build AI

AI that shows its work

We use AI where it is good: reading documents, finding the right fields and explaining in plain language. We keep it away from the parts that must be exact. Rules written in code compute the numbers, every result shows its source, and a person makes the decision.

01

Numbers come from code

AI reads and explains. Every figure you see is computed by rules written in code, which we can test and you can trace.

02

Sources are shown

Each result points to the line, field or document it came from. If a result cannot show its source, it does not ship.

03

People decide

Our tools flag, explain and point. The reviewer, the family or the trader makes the call, and can disagree.

The pattern our AI products follow. In Zinvera, the review engine's rules run today; AI document reading is in development for the first pilot release.

Testing

Measured before we rely on it

In Zinvera, we test against synthetic cases with known answers and check the results before we rely on them. We do not quote those figures in public, because they say nothing about client returns.

Data

Pilots run on synthetic data only, and we do not use client data to train AI models.
