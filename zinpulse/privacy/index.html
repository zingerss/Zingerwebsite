ZinPulse Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

ZinPulse is a workout and coaching app made by Zinger Software Solutions, Inc ("we", "us"), 226 Great Bynum Drive, Apex, NC 27523, USA. This policy explains what data the app uses, where it goes, and the choices you have. Contact: support@zingersoftwaresolutions.com.

The short version

  • ZinPulse has no accounts. Your workouts, profile, check-ins and photos are stored on your phone.
  • AI coach features send the information they need to Anthropic's Claude AI through our server, only after you allow it.
  • We don't sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps.

1. Data stored on your device

The app stores the following locally on your phone: your profile (goal, experience, equipment, schedule, joints that need care, and optionally age, sex, height and weight), routines, programs, workout history, body check-ins (weight, estimates you confirm) and check-in photos. We do not have access to it. Uninstalling the app or using Settings → Erase all data deletes it. Exported backups are files you control; they exclude photos.

2. AI features (Claude by Anthropic)

AI features (program designer, sessions built for today, routine adjustments, coach chat and photo check-in analysis) are powered by Claude, an AI model provided by Anthropic, PBC. Before the first AI request the app asks for your permission. When you use an AI feature, the app sends what that feature needs, which can include your profile, recent workouts and routines, your message, and for photo analysis, the check-in photos you choose to analyse. These requests pass through our server (hosted on Cloudflare) to Anthropic, which processes them to return a result.

  • We do not store the content of AI requests or your photos on our servers.
  • Anthropic processes the data under its commercial terms and may retain it for a limited period for safety and abuse monitoring. Anthropic does not use this data to train its models. See anthropic.com/legal.
  • You can turn AI sharing off at any time in Settings → Data & AI. AI features then stop working; everything else keeps working on your phone.

3. Data our server handles

To run the AI service securely, our server processes technical data such as your IP address, an anonymous app-install identifier and request timestamps. We use it for rate limiting, security and troubleshooting, and keep operational logs for no more than 30 days.

4. Subscriptions

ZinPulse Pro is sold through Google Play or the Apple App Store. They handle payment; we never receive your card details. We use RevenueCat, Inc. to manage subscription status. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app-install identifier and your purchase records from the store.

5. What we don't do

No advertising. No selling or renting of personal data. No cross-app tracking. No analytics SDKs that profile you.

6. Health information

Workout, body-weight and body-composition information is sensitive, and so are any joints you tell ZinPulse need care. It stays on your device except when you send it to an AI feature as described in section 2. ZinPulse is not a medical service, and its estimates are not medical measurements.

6a. Google Health Connect and Apple Health

If you choose to connect Health Connect (Android) or Apple Health (iOS) in Settings, ZinPulse:

  • Writes the workouts you finish in ZinPulse (type, start and end time, estimated active calories) and the body weights you log in check-ins.
  • Reads your step count for today, your most recent sleep duration and your resting heart rate. These are used only on your phone to show today's activity and to adjust your readiness score.

Health data read from Health Connect or Apple Health stays on your device. It is not sent to our server or to the AI, not used for advertising, not sold, and not shared with anyone. You can disconnect at any time in Settings, and manage or revoke permissions in Health Connect or the Health app. Data ZinPulse already wrote there stays there until you delete it.

ZinPulse's use of information received from Health Connect adheres to the Health Connect Permissions policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from Apple HealthKit is used only to provide the features above, in line with Apple's HealthKit guidelines.

7. Children

ZinPulse is not intended for children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA and UK). Photo check-ins are limited to users 18 and over.

8. Your rights

Because your data is stored on your device, you can view, export or delete it directly in the app. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have rights to access, delete or object to processing of personal data we handle. Contact us at the address above to exercise them. We don't sell personal information.

9. International transfers

Our server and Anthropic may process data in the United States and other countries. Where required, transfers rely on appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

10. Changes

We'll update this page when the policy changes, and tell you in the app for significant changes.
