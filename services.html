For CPA Firms: Zinvera

Zinvera is our review tool for CPA firms that review complex individual returns. It sits after the firm's tax software and before sign-off, and it prepares, changes and files nothing. In the pilot, reviewers work through pre-computed findings on synthetic case files; the rule set's tax positions are not yet signed off by a CPA.

Who It Is For

  • Firms reviewing 1040s with several K-1s
  • Returns with a state pass-through entity tax
  • Brokerage accounts with many lots
  • More than one draft before sign-off
  • Firms using offshore or AI-assisted preparation

What the Pilot Involves

  • Fictional case files only; no client data
  • Runs in the browser; nothing to install
  • Your view on each finding, at your own pace
  • No charge; you can withdraw at any time
