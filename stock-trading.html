⚡ Options Scalp Panel

A fast, single-screen options trading panel with risk built in, not bolted on.

  • Hard per-trade size and risk caps that cannot be overridden
  • Daily loss stops and profit goals that lock new entries automatically, while exits are never blocked
  • Stops placed on the chart at the stock's own levels, then translated into the option
  • Every order protected by a bracket, with live protection status on each position
  • A full trade journal and market tape recorded for review

More of What We're Building

📈

Swing Levels Engine

A nightly engine that maps support, resistance and structure across 230+ stocks, applies strict gates (reward-to-risk, earnings, liquidity), then watches the qualifying setups live and alerts when price confirms.

💵

Income Radar

Scans for cash-secured put opportunities below real support levels, labels every idea with its earnings date, cross-checked across sources, and tracks each one to its outcome.

🔎

Momentum Screener

A real-time screener for fast-moving stocks, with news and dilution labels, halt-risk flags and position sizing built into every alert.

The Grading Lab

Every alert any of our tools produces is logged and graded forward against random entries. If a signal doesn't beat chance, we say so, and we change it or drop it.
