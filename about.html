About

Practitioners building tools that show their work

Zinger Software Solutions is an AI product studio. We build Zinvera, AI-assisted review for CPA firms; ZinPulse, an AI workout coach that shows its work; ZinTrading, risk-first tools for disciplined traders; and Zinn, one assistant across our products. Since 2013 we have also helped biotech, pharmaceutical and CRO teams deliver submission-ready clinical data through statistical programming, biostatistics support, technical staffing and strategic consulting.

Our story

Our Story

Zinger Software Solutions began in 2013 with a simple mission: provide high-quality clinical programming services to biotechnology and pharmaceutical companies. For more than 12 years we have delivered SDTM datasets, ADaM analyses and TLF outputs for studies ranging from early-phase biotech trials to large global submissions.

What clients rely on us for: Experienced people who understand CDISC standards, regulatory expectations and real study timelines — whether that means placing a programmer on your team, owning a deliverable end to end, or advising on how to structure your programming and biostatistics work.

Over time our work has grown beyond hands-on programming. Today we provide technical staffing for clinical programming and biostatistics roles, statistical programming (SDTM, ADaM, TLF and eCTD submission packages) with biostatistics support, strategic consulting on standards and submission readiness, and custom solutions built around a client's specific workflow.

Alongside our services business, we are building ZinTrading — trading software currently in private development — and Zinvera, a review tool for CPA firms that is in an invitation-only pilot. Both reflect the same approach we bring to client work: practical, transparent and built by people who use it.

We remain a focused team based in Apex, North Carolina. Our advantage is not size — it is the practitioner knowledge and accountability behind every engagement.

Company Evolution

  1. 2013

    Company Founded

  2. 2018

    TLF & Submission Expertise

    Became known for submission-ready TLF creation and regulatory support, developing standardized processes that consistently delivered regulatory-compliant outputs ahead of deadlines.

  3. 2025

    ZinTrading Development Begins

    Began building ZinTrading, our trading software, as a separate effort alongside our core services business.

  4. 2026

    Zinvera Pilot Being Prepared

    Started Zinvera, a review tool for CPA firms, and began preparing an invitation-only pilot on synthetic case files. We are working through the rule set's tax positions with a practising CPA; they are not yet signed off.
