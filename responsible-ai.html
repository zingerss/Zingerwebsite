Responsible AI

Responsible AI, in plain words

Three commitments, and what they mean for your data.

Synthetic data only in pilots

Our pilots run on synthetic, fictional case files. The Zinvera pilot accepts no client data.

No training on client data

We do not use client data to train AI models.

Numbers from code

AI reads and explains. Rules in code compute the numbers, sources are shown, and people decide.

How data is handled

  • Zinvera pilot. Reviewers work on synthetic case files with pre-computed findings. No client data is accepted. The only thing that leaves a reviewer's browser is the feedback they choose to save.
  • Before any real data. Before a Zinger product accepts real client data, we will publish how it is stored, who processes it, how long it is kept and how to delete it, on this page.
  • Contact form. Our contact form opens your own email app; the site does not collect form data itself.
  • Honest Odds runs on its own site, honest-odds.com; see that site for how it handles information.
  • XSLT Bridge sends nothing to us automatically. See the XSLT Bridge privacy policy.
