ZinPulse: a workout coach that shows its work

Every score has a reason you can see, and every plan fits your kit, your time and your joints.

ZinPulse is a training app for Android and iPhone from Zinger Software Solutions. Tell it your goal, your equipment, the days you can train and any joints that need care, and it builds your week as Day 1, Day 2, Day 3, then coaches each session with weight targets, timers and voice cues.

Readiness, program match and weight targets are worked out on your phone from your own training; tap any score to see how it was calculated. Zinn, Zinger's AI assistant, designs custom programs, builds sessions on the spot and answers questions in ZinPulse Pro.

The app

What a session looks like

A daily briefing, a target for every lift, a timer that runs the intervals, and an analysis at the end.

Screens from the test build with demo training data.

What it does

Built around how you actually train

Free: programs, quick sessions, timers, tracking and every on-device score. ZinPulse Pro adds Zinn's AI features.

A plan for your week

Ready-made programs ranked by how well they match your goal, level, schedule and equipment, run as Day 1, Day 2, Day 3. Miss a day and the next one simply moves up.

Today's session, your way

Strength, cardio, HIIT, core or mobility, fitted to the minutes you have and how you feel. Swap any exercise for one that suits your kit.

Joint care

Flag a sore knee, shoulder or lower back. Moves that load it get a caution and a gentler swap, their weight is never raised automatically, and a joint warm-up is built for you. Not medical advice.

Timers and a voice coach

Rest timers, Tabata and EMOM intervals, a breathing guide between sets and spoken cues, with alerts when the phone is locked.

Progress you can feel

Strength and body-weight trends, weekly streaks, badges, a monthly recap and the total weight you lifted, compared with something you can picture.

Android first

Health Connect

Optionally save workouts and weight to Health Connect, and let sleep and resting heart rate feed your readiness score. Apple Health is planned for the iPhone release.

Worked out on your phone

Scores with the maths attached

The numbers in ZinPulse come from code running on your phone, using your own training, not from an AI guess.

  • Readiness: recovery time, this week's training load and, if you connect it, sleep and resting heart rate
  • Program match: points for goal, experience, schedule, equipment and joint care, added up in front of you
  • Weight targets: the smallest step up when you hit the top of the rep range on every set, held steady on flagged joints
  • Session score: sets completed, progress against last time, and personal bests
Zinn in ZinPulse

The AI part, and its limits

With ZinPulse Pro, Zinn designs multi-week programs, builds a session for right now, adjusts any routine, estimates body composition from optional check-in photos and answers training questions from your own logs.

Zinn runs on Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic. ZinPulse asks before anything is sent, and only what that feature needs leaves the phone. Zinn respects the joints you flag and says plainly when something sounds like an injury. It is a coach, not a clinician.

About Zinn

Your data

No account. Your training stays on your phone.

Workouts, check-ins and photos are stored on the device. Health Connect data is used on the phone only and never sent to the AI. No ads, no selling data, no cross-app tracking.
