Frequently Asked Questions

What is Zinvera and how do I join the pilot?

Zinvera is our review tool for CPA firms that review complex individual returns. It is in an invitation-only pilot: reviewers work through pre-computed findings on synthetic case files, no client data is accepted, and the rule set's tax positions are not yet signed off by a CPA. Select "Zinvera — early access for CPA firms" in the form above, or read more on the Zinvera page.

What is ZinTrading and when will it open?

ZinTrading is our trading software, currently in private development and not yet open to members. Select "ZinTrading — notify me when it opens" in the form above and we'll let you know when it launches. ZinTrading is not financial advice.

Is Zinni available yet?

Not yet. Zinni is in development for Zinvera's first pilot release and planned for ZinTrading and Honest Odds. See the Zinni page.

What engagement models do you offer?

We work on a contract, contract-to-hire, or full-time placement basis for staffing, and on a project or ongoing-support basis for statistical programming and consulting. We'll recommend the model that fits your study and budget.

How quickly can you staff a role?

It depends on the role, seniority and therapeutic area. Tell us what you need through the form above and we'll respond within 24 hours with realistic timing and next steps.

What statistical programming work do you cover?

SAS-based SDTM and ADaM dataset development, TLF programming, validation, and eCTD submission packages, with biostatistics support when your study needs it.

What makes Zinger different from other providers?

More than 12 years of hands-on clinical programming experience and a boutique approach — you work directly with experienced people who take ownership of your deliverables.

How do I schedule a strategy consultation?

Fill out the contact form above and select "Strategic Consulting" as your primary interest. We'll respond within 24 hours to schedule a call at your convenience.
