ZinTrading

Our trading platform, currently in private development.

The edge is in the process and the risk control, not in a magic signal.

⚡ Options Scalp Panel

A single-screen options panel with hard per-trade risk caps that cannot be overridden, daily loss stops, and bracket protection on every order.

📈 Swing Levels Engine

Maps support, resistance and structure across 230+ stocks nightly, applies strict reward-to-risk, earnings and liquidity gates, and alerts when price confirms.

💵 Income Radar

Finds cash-secured put ideas below real support levels, labels each with a cross-checked earnings date, and tracks every idea to its outcome.

🔎 Momentum Screener

A real-time screener for fast-moving stocks, with news and dilution labels, halt-risk flags and position sizing in every alert.

In development

🧪 The Grading Lab

Every alert is logged and graded forward against random entries. If a signal doesn't beat chance, we say so, then change it or drop it.
