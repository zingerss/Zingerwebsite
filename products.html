Zinvera

Invitation-only pilot

AI-assisted tax return review for CPA firms

Zinvera is designed to read the draft return beside the K-1s, the broker statements and last year's return, carry each partner's basis forward year to year, follow each finding from one draft to the next, and show the reviewer the line each finding came from and the authority behind it. It sits after the firm's tax software and before sign-off, and it prepares, changes and files nothing.

  • Basis continuity ledger for each partnership and S corporation
  • Cross-document checks against K-1s, broker composites, W-2s and the prior-year return
  • Draft 1 → Draft 2 tracking: fixed, not fixed, fixed incorrectly, regression, new
  • Form 8879 currency check against the current draft
  • Evidence and citations on each finding, plus a planning preview

AI-assisted features (document reading, judgment flags, plain-language explanations, and Zinn, an assistant that answers questions about the return) are in development for the first pilot release; the review engine's tax rules run today, and every number stays in code.

ZinTrading

Private development
Risk-first trading software

ZinTrading is a set of tools built around one idea: the edge is in the process and the risk control, not in a magic signal. Risk caps, daily stops and protection checks come first. Every alert is graded against random entries, and we will publish the results honestly as tools are released.

  • Options Scalp Panel with hard, non-overridable risk caps
  • Swing Levels Engine covering 230+ stocks nightly
  • Income Radar for cash-secured put ideas below real support
  • Momentum Screener with dilution and halt-risk flags (in development)
  • Grading Lab that tests every signal against random entries

ZinPulse

In testing

An AI workout coach that shows its work

ZinPulse plans your week as Day 1, Day 2, Day 3, sets a weight target for every lift, runs your rest and interval timers, and swaps moves that would aggravate a joint you have flagged. Readiness, program match and weight targets are worked out on the phone from your own training, and tapping any score shows how it was calculated.

  • Ready-made programs ranked for your goal, level, schedule and equipment
  • Sessions fitted to the time you have and how you feel today
  • Joint care: cautions, gentler swaps and a joint warm-up
  • Timers, a voice coach, streaks, badges and a monthly recap
  • Optional Health Connect sync for workouts, weight, sleep and resting heart rate

With ZinPulse Pro, Zinn designs custom programs, builds sessions on the spot, estimates body composition from optional check-in photos and answers training questions. Zinn runs on Claude by Anthropic and only after you allow it.

XSLT Bridge

Launching November 2026
Chrome and Edge extension for legacy XML / MSXML web apps

Chrome and Edge remove built-in XSLT by default from version 158. XSLT Bridge keeps XML reports, Internet Explorer era MSXML applications and CDISC define.xml files working, inside the browser, on the sites and folders you approve. It sends nothing to us automatically.

  • XSLT restored: XML pages with stylesheets and the XSLTProcessor API
  • MSXML compatibility for insurance, claims and enterprise apps
  • CDISC define.xml from web servers and local submission folders
  • Group Policy and Intune deployment, offline license keys
  • Insurance security pack and GxP validation pack
