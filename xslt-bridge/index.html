XSLT Bridge · launching November 2026

XSLT Bridge

Keep XSLT and Internet Explorer era (MSXML) web applications working in Chrome and Edge after the browsers remove XSLT.

Chrome and Edge remove their built-in XSLT support by default starting with version 158 (November 2026). Pages that turn XML into HTML with XSLT, and apps written for Internet Explorer's MSXML, stop working. XSLT Bridge restores them on the sites and folders you approve.

XSLT restored

XML pages with a stylesheet render again, and the standard XSLTProcessor API keeps working, using a libxslt engine that runs inside the browser.

MSXML compatibility

ActiveXObject, DOMDocument, selectNodes, transformNode and XSLTemplate for Internet Explorer era insurance, claims and enterprise apps (paid edition).

Pharma: define.xml

CDISC define.xml files render from web servers and from approved local submission folders, with their original stylesheets.

Enterprise ready

Group Policy and Intune deployment, approved-site lists, offline license keys, an insurance security pack and a GxP validation pack.

Privacy and security

XSLT Bridge sends nothing to us automatically: no telemetry, no analytics and no license server. It runs only on the websites and local folders that you or your administrator approve. See the XSLT Bridge privacy policy.
