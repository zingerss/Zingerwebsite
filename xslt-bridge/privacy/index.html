XSLT Bridge Privacy Policy

How the XSLT Bridge browser extension handles your data.

Effective date: October 18, 2026

Who we are. XSLT Bridge is published by Zinger Software Solutions, Inc., Apex, North Carolina, USA ("we"). Contact: privacy@zingersoftwaresolutions.com.

Summary

XSLT Bridge does not collect, transmit, sell or share your data. It has no analytics, no telemetry, no advertising, no crash reporting and no license server. It works inside your browser, only on the websites and local folders that you or your organization approve.

What the extension processes, and where

  • Content of approved web pages and local files. On a website or local folder you or your administrator approved, the extension reads the page's XML and the stylesheets it uses and turns them into the page you see, inside your browser. This content is not sent to us.
  • Settings. Your approved sites, your organization's policy settings and, if you entered one, your license key are stored in your browser's extension storage on your device.
  • Diagnostic log. The extension keeps a short log of its own errors and warnings in your browser's extension storage (at most 1,000 entries). Web addresses are stored without query strings, fragments or passwords, and license keys are removed. Error messages can quote parts of the page or document that caused them. You can clear the log on the settings page; it is deleted when you remove the extension.

What leaves your device

Nothing is sent to us automatically. Network requests happen only in these cases:

  • The approved websites load as they normally would, and the extension loads the stylesheets and documents those pages refer to, from the same servers (or servers that allow it).
  • If your organization's administrator turns on the optional "cross-origin stylesheets" setting, the extension fetches XML stylesheets from the servers the administrator names, with your browser's sign-in cookies for those servers.
  • The transformed page can load images, scripts or other content from the addresses the website's own stylesheet puts in it, as any web page does.
  • Your browser updates the extension through the Chrome Web Store or Microsoft Edge Add-ons.
  • Only if you choose to: the paid edition can export a diagnostic file. If you send it to us for support, we receive that file. Please look at it before sending; it can contain extracts of the documents that caused errors. We use it only to answer your support request and delete it within 90 days after the request is closed.

What we do not do

We do not sell or transfer user data to third parties, do not use it for purposes unrelated to the extension's single purpose, and do not use it to determine creditworthiness or for lending. We do not use cookies or tracking in the extension.

Purchases

If your organization buys a license, for example through AWS Marketplace, we receive the business contact details needed to issue the license key: the purchasing AWS account identifier, the company name and an administrator email address. We use them only to issue, renew and support the license, keep them while the license is active and as required for our business and tax records, and do not sell them. This is separate from the extension, which never sends us anything automatically.

Children

The extension is a business tool and is not directed at children.

Changes

We will post changes to this policy at this address and update the effective date.
